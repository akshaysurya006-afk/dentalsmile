Our Practice

A boutique practice on Fifth Avenue

Founded in 1991, BrightSmile Dental has grown into one of New York's most respected aesthetic and implant dentistry practices. We combine advanced technology with an artistic eye to deliver natural, lasting results.

Where artistry meets science

Every treatment plan begins with listening. We take the time to understand your goals, your concerns, and your lifestyle — then design a plan that fits you.

Our Midtown Manhattan office is equipped with digital imaging, 3D planning tools, and an on-site laboratory, allowing us to handle complex cases with precision and efficiency.
